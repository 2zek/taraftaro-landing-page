Account Deletion & Data Erasure Policy

Last Updated: August 28, 2026

At Taraftaro, we respect your privacy and data ownership rights. You have the right to permanently delete your account and all associated personal data at any time.

⚠️ Warning: This Action is Permanent

Once your account is deleted, your profile data, prediction history, badges, and all associated personal records will be permanently erased.

How to Delete Your Account

Option 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

Open the Taraftaro mobile app and navigate to Profile > Settings > Delete Account. Follow the confirmation prompt to immediately and permanently erase your account.

Option 2: Email Deletion Request

If you no longer have access to the app or have uninstalled it, send an email from your registered email address to info@taraftaro.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request". Your request will be processed within 24 hours.

Data Types Erased

  • Identity & Profile Data: Full name, username, email address, profile picture, and bio.
  • Fan & Interaction Data: Followed clubs/players, matchday predictions, and poll voting history.
  • Device Identifiers: Push notification tokens (FCM/APNs) and access logs.

Data Retention & Erasure Timeline

Account records are deleted from our active database instantly upon request. Data retained in secure backups is purged automatically within a maximum of 30 days.
