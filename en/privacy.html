Privacy Policy & Data Protection (GDPR)

Last Updated: August 28, 2026

1. Introduction & Data Controller

At Taraftaro ("Company", "Platform", "We", "Us"), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and website, compliant with GDPR, KVKK, Google Play Developer Policies, and Apple App Store Review Guidelines.

Intellectual Property & Sports Data Disclaimer: Taraftaro is an independent fan community and sports engagement platform. All club crests, team names, stadium imagery, league, and federation trademarks (such as UEFA, FIFA, TFF, Premier League, etc.) displayed on the platform are the property of their respective owners. These assets are utilized solely for informational, sports reporting, and fan community engagement under the Fair Use doctrine. Taraftaro is not officially endorsed by, affiliated with, or sponsored by any sports club or governing federation.

2. Data We Collect

  • Account & Identity Data: Name, email address, username, profile picture, and bio.
  • Authentication Data: Encrypted password hash, verification status, or tokens from Social Logins (Google Sign-In, Apple ID).
  • Fan Preferences: Supported primary club, followed sports, leagues, teams, and players.
  • Platform Interactions: Live digital tribune reactions, lineup voting, fan predictions, poll votes, shared posts, and comments.
  • Device & Security Data: IP address, device model, OS version, push notification tokens (FCM/APNs), and anonymous diagnostic logs.

3. Mobile Device Permissions

  • Camera & Photo Library: Used strictly for profile photo uploads and attaching media to fan community posts/comments. We never access other files on your device.
  • Push Notifications: Used to deliver real-time match scores, goal alerts, official starting lineups, and fan interaction notifications. You can disable this anytime in your device settings.

4. How We Use Your Data

  • To deliver a personalized club-themed fan experience (ClubTheme) and tailored sports news feeds,
  • To operate the live matchday digital tribune and gamified prediction leaderboards,
  • To ensure community safety and prevent spam, bots, harassment, and malicious activity,
  • To maintain platform security, optimize performance, and resolve technical issues.

5. Data Sharing & Third Parties

Taraftaro does not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties under any circumstances. Data is shared only with technical service providers essential for app operation (Hetzner cloud infrastructure, Firebase FCM, Apple APNs, Centrifugo WebSocket) and law enforcement where strictly required by law.

6. Account Deletion & Data Erasure (Google Play Compliant)

You have the full right to delete your account and all associated data at any time:

  • Within the App: Navigate to Profile > Settings > Delete Account to permanently erase your account instantly.
  • Via Web / Email: Visit our Account Deletion Page or email us at info@taraftaro.com.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Our services are not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. Any such data detected is deleted immediately.

8. Your Rights (GDPR & KVKK)

You have the right to access, rectify, port, or request the erasure of your personal data, as well as restrict or object to its processing by contacting us.
